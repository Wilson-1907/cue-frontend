About CUE

We build practical IoT tools that watch water flow, catch leaks early, and help communities conserve every drop.

Who we are

CUE (smart water monitoring) is a Kenyan-built platform that combines Arduino/IoT sensing with a live dashboard, leak detection, SMS alerts, and automated valve control. Our official brand mark — CUE — stands for clear, usable water intelligence for homes, farms, and institutions.

From the pipe to the phone, CUE turns silent water loss into visible action: live L/min readings, locus estimates for repairs, and fast shut-off when a leak is confirmed.

At a glance

  • Inlet (S1) & outlet (S2) flow monitoring
  • Automated leak confirmation & valve close
  • SMS alerts with repair PIN / SEARCH guidance
  • Live dashboard, trends, and CUE Assistance

Problem

Undetected leaks and late manual response waste scarce water and raise costs. Many sites have no affordable way to compare inlet vs outlet flow or shut a line automatically when something goes wrong.

Mission

Deliver an affordable, reliable, user-friendly monitoring and control system that detects leaks early, prevents wastage, and makes conservation practical for everyday operators.

Vision

Accessible IoT water management supporting SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation — so communities protect every drop with smart, local infrastructure.

Core values

Reliability

Stable sensing, clear alerts, and valve actions that work when it counts.

Affordability

Hardware and software designed to be adopted beyond labs and pilots.

Transparency

Live dashboards, honest leak locus guidance, and SMS you can act on.

Stewardship

Water is shared — we build tools that reduce waste and protect supply.

What we deliver

A complete path from kit to cloud: Arduino firmware, serial/Wi‑Fi telemetry, Django API, operator dashboard, leak SMS protocol, and CUE Assistance (text & voice help). Operators can monitor flow, confirm leaks, close valves, and reach support via the contact icons.
