About Us

Smarter water monitoring for every drop that matters.

CUE builds affordable IoT water-flow monitoring and automated control for homes, farms, schools, and institutions. We help people see leaks early, stop waste automatically, and manage water with clear live data ? not guesswork.

The problem we solve

Hidden pipe leaks, delayed detection, and manual shut-offs waste water and money. Many sites still lack simple, local tools that watch inlet/outlet flow, raise alerts, and close a valve before loss grows.

Our mission

To design and deploy an affordable, reliable, user-friendly real-time water monitoring and control system that detects leaks early, prevents wastage, and supports efficient conservation for households, farms, and institutions.

Our vision

Accessible, IoT-driven water management that supports Sustainable Development Goal 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation) ? empowering communities with practical infrastructure for every drop.

Core values

Reliability
Sensors, alerts, and valve actions you can trust.
Affordability
Practical hardware that communities can adopt.
Transparency
Live flow data, clear dashboards, actionable SMS.
Stewardship
Conserve water and protect shared resources.

Our Service

Smart Monitoring & Automated Control for Every Pipeline

Household Flow Monitoring

Real-time tracking of domestic water flow rates (1 to $30~L/min$) and cumulative volume on 0.5-inch and 0.75-inch lines to prevent overbilling and undetected leaks.

Agricultural & Farm Irrigation

Automated valve scheduling and threshold-based flow control for small-scale farms to eliminate over-irrigation and reduce water wastage by up to 20?30%.

Institutional & School Systems

Cost-effective pipeline monitoring and usage accountability tailored for schools, public institutions, and community water setups.

Automated Leak & Valve Shut-Off

Instant anomaly detection in Arduino firmware that triggers a 12V solenoid valve via a relay module whenever flow exceeds safe thresholds.

Cloud IoT Dashboards

Wireless telemetry via ESP8266/ESP32 WiFi modules to platforms like ThingSpeak, Firebase, or Blynk for live charts, alerts, and remote valve control.

STEM & Lab Prototyping

Open-source hardware and firmware architecture built for university research, laboratory test rigs, and hands-on embedded systems education.
